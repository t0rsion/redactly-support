Redactly: Hide Screenshot Info

How do I hide something?

Import a screenshot or photo, then drag across the part you want to cover. You can also tap "Add adjustable area" and position it precisely with the sliders. Undo removes the last area.

How do I cover small details?

Pinch to zoom in, up to 8×, or double-tap a spot to zoom to 250% there. Move around with two fingers. A one-finger drag still draws a cover, exactly on what's under your finger. You can also use the zoom buttons under the image, and Fit to go back. The exported image is always full size.

Can someone remove the cover from an exported image?

No. The export is a new, flattened PNG. Covered pixels are replaced with solid colour, not blurred or layered, so the hidden content isn't in the file. The original photo's metadata, such as location, isn't copied either.

Does Redactly find private details automatically?

No. You choose what to cover, so check the image carefully before sharing it.

Where is my original image?

Unchanged in Photos or Files. Redactly doesn't save your images. If you close the app before exporting, your edits are gone.

Is there a size limit?

Images up to 40 megapixels and 20,000 pixels per side are supported, at full resolution.
