Privacy Policy

Apps by Tor Andreas Sandve Kringeland · Effective 5 November 2026

Short version: our apps do not collect any data about you. They have no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs, and they don't send your content to any server.

What stays on your device

Everything you create or import is processed on your iPhone or iPad. If an app saves your work, it stores it in that app's private storage on your device. That storage is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. We cannot see that data.

Photos, files and camera

When you choose a photo or file, iOS gives the app access to that item only. Apps that offer camera, microphone or speech features ask for your permission first and use those features only for the action you started. Speech recognition is performed on device, and no recording is sent to Apple or to us.

Exports and sharing

Exported files go only where you choose to save or share them, using the standard iOS share and save options.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Our apps collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions: contact@vixe.re.

Redactly: Hide Screenshot Info

Redactly holds the image you're editing in memory only and doesn't save it. When you export, Redactly creates a new PNG in which the covered areas are replaced with solid pixels. The original photo's metadata, such as location, is not copied into the export. Your original image in Photos or Files is never changed.
